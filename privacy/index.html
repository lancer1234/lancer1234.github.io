政策

Miles is a local-first performance logging tool. We do not create accounts, run ads, or track you with your data.Miles 是本機優先的性能紀錄工具。我們不建立帳號、不投放廣告，也不以你的資料進行追蹤。

01

No accounts不建立帳號

No name, email, or phone number is required. You never trade personal data for product access.不要求姓名、電子郵件或電話；你不需要用個人資料換取產品功能。

02

Your data stays yours資料留在你手上

Records stay on your device by default. iCloud backups go only to your private database.測試紀錄預設儲存在裝置；iCloud 備份只會進入你自己的私人資料庫。

03

No tracking沒有追蹤

No third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. We do not sell or share your records.不使用第三方廣告、分析或追蹤 SDK，也不出售或分享測試資料。

Scope適用範圍

This policy applies to the Miles iOS app ("Miles") provided by MAKOTO LAB. By using Miles, you agree to the data practices described in this policy.本政策適用於由 MAKOTO LAB 提供的 Miles iOS App（以下稱「Miles」）。使用 Miles 即表示你同意本政策所述的資料處理方式。

How data is used資料如何使用

Miles uses the following device data to show live speed and provide the tests you initiate:Miles 使用下列裝置資料提供即時速度，以及由你主動啟動的測試與紀錄功能：

  • Precise location: Measures speed, distance, start and end points, and generates a minimal route line.精確位置：用來測量速度、距離、起終點與產生簡約路線線稿。
  • Motion data: Calculates acceleration and G-force curves, and helps identify test status.動作感測資料：用來計算加速度與 G 值曲線，並協助辨識測試狀態。
  • Test records: Results, time-series samples, G-force curves, route and available altitude samples, vehicle profiles, test-day notes and available weather snapshots are stored on your device. Run analysis is performed on-device.測試紀錄：包含成績、時間序列、G 值曲線、路線與可用的海拔樣本、車輛資料、測試日筆記及可用的天氣快照，儲存在你的裝置中；單趟資料解讀在裝置上進行。
  • Photo Library Add permission: Used only when you choose to save a result card to Photos.相簿新增權限：僅在你選擇將成績圖卡儲存至相簿時使用。

Apple Maps and weatherApple 地圖與天氣服務

When you select a map view or map-backed card, Miles requests map imagery from Apple through MapKit for the route area. When you arm a test and suitable location information is available, Miles may request nearby conditions from Apple Weather through WeatherKit using that location. These requests are made to Apple to provide the selected map or weather feature; your complete run history is not sent with a weather request. MAKOTO LAB does not operate a server that receives your test routes or weather requests. Apple's services are subject to Apple's privacy policy.當你選擇地圖檢視或地圖圖卡時，Miles 會透過 MapKit 向 Apple 請求路線區域的圖資。當你準備測試且有合適定位資料時，Miles 可能使用該位置，透過 WeatherKit 向 Apple Weather 查詢附近天氣。這些請求用於提供地圖或天氣功能；天氣請求不會附帶完整歷史紀錄。MAKOTO LAB 沒有接收測試路線或天氣請求的自有伺服器。Apple 服務適用 Apple 隱私權政策。

Weather conditions, their query location and request-budget state may be cached locally to reduce repeated network requests. A saved run keeps its weather snapshot and source time. Offline or unavailable weather does not prevent testing. Unlike an outline card, a map-backed export reveals the route's geographic context. Review its contents before sharing; the app shares an image only when you choose a share or save action.天氣內容、查詢位置及請求額度狀態可能在本機快取，以減少重複連線；成績會保留當時的天氣快照與資料時間。離線或無法取得天氣時仍可測試。地圖圖卡與簡約線稿不同，會揭露路線的地理背景，分享前請自行確認內容；只有你選擇分享或儲存時，App 才會輸出圖片。

Private iCloud backupiCloud 私人備份

If you choose iCloud backup, Miles stores your history and garage in the private CloudKit database of your own iCloud account. It exists solely to restore your records across your devices; MAKOTO LAB does not view, sell, or use this data for analytics, advertising, or tracking.若你選擇使用 iCloud 備份，Miles 會將你的歷史紀錄與車庫儲存在你自己 iCloud 帳號的私人 CloudKit 資料庫中。此備份用於你在自己的裝置間還原紀錄；MAKOTO LAB 不會查看、出售或將這些資料用於分析、廣告或追蹤。

What we do not do我們不會做的事

  • We do not create user accounts or request your name, email address, or phone number.不建立使用者帳號，也不要求你提供姓名、電子郵件或電話。
  • We do not use third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.不使用第三方廣告、分析或追蹤 SDK。
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your performance, location, or route records.不出售、出租或分享你的性能、位置或路線紀錄。

Your control你的控制權

You can change location and Photos permissions at any time in iOS Settings. You manage history stored on your device within the app, while iCloud backups are managed through your Apple ID and iCloud settings.你可隨時在 iOS「設定」中變更位置與相簿權限。裝置上的歷史紀錄可由你在 App 中管理；iCloud 備份則受你的 Apple ID 與 iCloud 設定管理。

Children's privacy兒童隱私

Miles is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information.Miles 不以兒童為對象，且不會刻意蒐集可識別個人的資料。
