您好，我是汪誠宇 Danny。我長期深耕整合行銷、品牌公關與線下活動執行，擅長在預算或通路受限的情況下找到突破點。過去的背景橫跨電影劇組（魏德聖、蕭雅全導演團隊）、美僑協會（ACC）雙語調酒師，以及高端飾金與消費品牌的企劃營運。這讓我養成兩個關鍵習慣：對現場細節和受眾心理保持敏銳，同時用數據邏輯來檢視專案成效。

品牌公關、跨國邀約與異業聯名

在洲際聯合負責海外品牌（如法國 Bostik 與消費性 3C 配件）的在台公關，包含全英文信件對接、確認原廠 Brand Guideline、撰寫中文官方新聞稿與媒體監測；並在公司產品線調整時，主導經銷夥伴的公關說明與 CRM 維護。

也曾主導台韓文化交流專案，引進韓國 Model 與創作者來台，負責外籍人士工作證申請、合約談判、全英語現場接待與拍攝行程安排，並在門市品牌轉型時重新梳理演出的標準作業流程（SOP）。此外，曾推動品牌與當代書法家跨界合作、統籌名廚聯名專案，並執行金車茶飲大型派樣與 Maybelline Threads 素人口碑擴散。

實體活動、在地 SEO 與門市體驗

在精品飾金產業面對主流廣告平台關鍵字阻擋時，我將重心轉向天母商圈在地經營。透過「聖誕盲盒」與「新春籤詩」異業打卡活動，引導客人留下帶圖五星好評，在零廣告費下帶動 70% 以上新客入店，並讓店家在短時間內衝上天母 Google 地圖排名首位。

在活動與營運端，我也主導節慶主題活動、VIP 包廂推廣，以及神秘客訪查制度，從消費者第一視角找出動線與服務痛點，讓企劃不只停留在提案，而能真正回到現場驗證。

數位工具、影像與跨文化溝通

取得 Google & Coursera 數位行銷與電商認證，並透過 CTR、CAC、ROAS 等指標評估活動；也曾利用 AI 工具輔助開發門市金價即時試算 App，簡化店內報價流程。

影像方面，我曾獨立完成短片《我的夢想》的企劃、拍攝與剪輯。加上美國一年交換求學，以及在美僑協會服務外籍會員的經驗，讓我能以英語進行日常與商務溝通，也習慣在不同文化背景與高壓現場之間快速切換。

我相信數據提供方向，而人性與現場細節讓策略更有溫度。我不只做策略規劃，也重視親力親為的落地執行；不論是品牌公關、實體活動或影像製作，我在意的都是如何把策略真正做成可以被人感受到的體驗。

I am a hands-on Marketing and PR Specialist experienced in campaign execution, media communications, and offline activations. My professional background spans film production with Taiwanese directors Wei Te-sheng and Hsiao Ya-chuan, bilingual guest relations at the American Club Taipei (ACC), and integrated marketing across luxury retail, consumer goods, and hospitality. I bring an operational approach to problem-solving, combining front-line empathy with structured, data-informed execution.

Brand PR & International Coordination

I have managed communications for international accounts, including Bostik France and consumer-electronics accessory brands, handling English correspondence, brand compliance, press releases, media monitoring, and partner communications.

I have also coordinated cross-border projects bringing South Korean creators and models to Taiwan, independently handling work permit filings, commercial terms, bilingual talent hosting, filming logistics, and schedule management. Other projects have included cross-industry collaborations with contemporary artists and culinary chefs, large-scale sampling for King Car, and community-driven advocacy for Maybelline on Threads.

Experiential Marketing & Local SEO

When advertising keyword restrictions limited acquisition in the gold-jewelry sector, I shifted growth toward local community activation in Tianmu. Through seasonal cross-promotions and verified photo reviews, the program drove more than 70% new-customer visits with zero ad spend and helped the business reach the top local Google Maps position.

I have also planned holiday activations, VIP venue promotions, and mystery-shopper programs designed to uncover service and flow issues from the customer’s perspective. For me, strategy only becomes meaningful when it survives contact with the real floor.

Digital Tools, Visual Production & Cross-Cultural Communication

I hold a Google & Coursera credential in digital marketing and e-commerce and use metrics such as CTR, CAC, and ROAS to evaluate campaigns. I have also used AI-assisted tools to build an internal real-time gold quotation app that simplified front-desk calculations and store operations.

On the visual side, I independently planned, shot, and edited the short film My Dream. A one-year exchange experience in the United States, together with serving international members at the American Club Taipei, strengthened my ability to communicate in English and work comfortably across different cultures and fast-moving environments.

I believe data provides direction, while human insight and on-the-ground details bring warmth to strategy. Whether I am working on brand PR, experiential campaigns, or visual production, I care about turning strategy into something people can actually feel.
