NOTE 001 / PERSONAL2026.09.16 / TAIPEI

Why I wanted a place to write again.為什麼我又想找一個地方寫東西。

A small beginning for a space somewhere between a work log, a lab notebook and a personal blog.給這個地方的一個小小開場：它大概會介於工作紀錄、實驗筆記和私人部落格之間。

我想要一個不是社群貼文、也不是履歷或作品集的地方。

有些事情做完之後，最後留下來的不只是成果。可能是某個專案裡很小的判斷、做壞之後才知道的事情，或是一個原本只是興趣，最後越做越完整的實驗。這些東西放在履歷裡太碎，放在社群上又太短。

所以這裡先留給它們。

之後可能會寫工作、行銷、公關，也會寫 MAKOTO LAB 裡那些奇怪的軟體和硬體實驗；可能是攝影，也可能只是某件最近一直在想的事。沒有打算把它變成一個很標準的「專業內容平台」，比較像是一份持續增加的個人檔案。

先從這裡開始。

I wanted a place that was neither a social post nor another extension of my résumé.

Some projects leave behind more than outcomes. There are small decisions, mistakes that only make sense afterwards, and experiments that begin as hobbies and slowly become real projects. They are too fragmented for a portfolio and usually too long for social media.

So this is where I want to keep them.

I will probably write about work, marketing and PR, but also the strange software and hardware experiments I build under MAKOTO LAB. Sometimes photography. Sometimes just an idea I have not been able to stop thinking about. I do not want this to become a polished “content platform.” It is closer to a personal archive that keeps growing.

This is a start.
